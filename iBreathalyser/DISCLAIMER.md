## 1. Purpose

iBreathalyser is provided strictly for **educational and informational
purposes**. It applies the Widmark formula to values you enter and returns
an estimate of BAC. It is not a medical device under EU Regulation 2017/745,
not approved by any health or law-enforcement authority, and not intended
for diagnosis or treatment of any condition.

---

## 2. The estimate is not accurate

Real BAC depends on variables the app cannot model, including:

- The actual ABV and serving size of each drink
- Whether drinks were consumed on an empty or full stomach
- Your individual metabolism (can differ ±30% from population averages)
- Liver function and any medication taken
- Genetic factors (e.g. ALDH2 variant)
- Body composition, hydration, sleep, and fatigue

The estimate may be **significantly lower or higher** than your true BAC.
An estimate labelled "Low" can correspond to a true BAC labelled "High".
