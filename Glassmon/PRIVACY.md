# Privacy Policy — Glassmon

**Last updated:** August 2026
**App name:** Glassmon
**Developer:** Juan Miguel Durán — juanmix13@hotmail.com

---

## 1. Plain-English summary

**Glassmon does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.** System metrics displayed in the app — CPU usage, memory, battery level, storage and network speed — are read locally in real time and never leave your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising network, and no third-party SDK.

If you uninstall the app, nothing remains anywhere.

---

## 2. What information the app handles

| Information | Purpose | Storage | Sent off-device? |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPU usage | Display CPU card | Memory only | **No** |
| RAM usage | Display Memory card | Memory only | **No** |
| Battery level & state | Display Battery card | Memory only | **No** |
| Storage capacity | Display Storage card | Memory only | **No** |
| Network interface counters | Display Network speed | Memory only | **No** |

---

## 3. What the app does NOT collect

- Personal identifiers (name, email, Apple ID, IDFA)
- Location data
- Health data from HealthKit
- Crash logs or telemetry
- Cookies or advertising identifiers
- Device identifiers of any kind

---

## 4. Third-party services

Glassmon does not integrate any third-party SDK. No Firebase, no Google Analytics, no Meta SDK, no AdMob, no crash reporting service. The app uses only Apple's public iOS frameworks and makes no outbound network requests.

---

## 5. Children

Glassmon is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. It does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

---

## 6. Apple's role

When you download from the App Store, Apple may receive standard anonymous download information. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy. The App Store listing reflects **"Data Not Collected"**.

---

## 7. Your GDPR rights

Because the app does not collect personal data, there is no data set against which to exercise GDPR rights. For questions, contact the developer at the email above. You may also contact the Spanish data protection authority: [aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es).
