# Privacy Policy — Fuentes CAM

**Last updated:** September 2026
**App name:** Fuentes CAM
**Developer:** Juan Miguel Durán — juanmix13@hotmail.com

---

## 1. Plain-English summary

**Fuentes CAM does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.** The app works entirely offline using a dataset bundled at install time. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising network, and no third-party SDK.

If you uninstall the app, nothing remains.

---

## 2. What information the app handles

| Information | Purpose | Storage | Sent off-device? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Location (GPS) | Show nearest fountains and compass direction | Memory only, never stored | **No** |
| Fountain dataset | Display drinking water sources near you | On-device bundle | **No** |

Location is used exclusively while the app is in the foreground and only to calculate distance and bearing to nearby fountains. It is never stored, logged, or transmitted.

---

## 3. What the app does NOT collect

- Personal identifiers (name, email, Apple ID, IDFA)
- Health data
- Crash logs or telemetry
- Cookies or advertising identifiers
- Usage patterns or analytics

---

## 4. Third-party services

Fuentes CAM does not integrate any third-party SDK. No Firebase, no Google Analytics, no Meta SDK, no AdMob.

The map view uses Apple MapKit, which is subject to Apple's own privacy policy. The app may optionally open Apple Maps for navigation; this is a separate app governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Fountain data is sourced from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0). No data is sent to OpenStreetMap servers during normal use.

---

## 5. Children

The app has no age restrictions and does not collect any data from any user, including children.

---

## 6. Apple's role

When you download from the App Store, Apple may receive standard anonymous download information. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy. The App Store listing reflects **"Data Not Collected"**.

---

## 7. Your GDPR rights

Because the app does not collect personal data, there is no data set against which to exercise GDPR rights. For questions, contact the developer at the email above. You may also contact the Spanish data protection authority: [aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es).
