# Disclaimer & Terms of Use — Fuentes CAM

**Last updated:** September 2026

> ## ⚠️ READ THIS BEFORE USING THE APPLICATION
> Fuentes CAM provides information about drinking water fountains sourced from
> OpenStreetMap volunteers. **The data may be incomplete, inaccurate, or
> out of date.** Always verify that water is safe to drink before consuming it,
> especially at springs and rural fountains.
>
> **When in doubt, do not drink the water.**

---

## 1. Purpose

Fuentes CAM is provided strictly for **informational purposes**. It helps
cyclists, hikers, and pedestrians locate potential drinking water sources
in the Community of Madrid. It is not an official water quality service
and makes no guarantee about the safety or availability of any listed fountain.

---

## 2. Data accuracy

Fountain data is sourced from OpenStreetMap, a collaborative mapping project
maintained by volunteers. This means:

- Fountains may have been removed, broken, or relocated since the data was last updated
- Some fountains marked as drinking water may not be potable
- Springs and natural water sources may carry contamination not visible to the naked eye
- Seasonal fountains may be dry outside their operating period
- The dataset is updated periodically but is not real-time

---

## 3. No guarantee of water safety

The app does not test, certify, or monitor water quality. The presence of a
fountain in the app does not imply that its water is safe to drink. Always:

- Check for official signage indicating potability
- Treat natural spring water if in doubt
- Carry your own water supply as a backup
